Privacy Policy for Solodoku

Effective Date: 7 October 2026

Matt Hodges ("we," "us," or "our") built the Solodoku app. Solodoku is free to download, with an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the full game. This page explains what information is involved when you use the app.

1. Summary

  • Solodoku has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask you to create an account with us.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your own device.
  • If you sign in to Google Play Games, your progress is also saved to your Google account by Google. We do not run a server and cannot see that data.
  • If you buy the unlock, Google handles the payment and a service called RevenueCat records that your purchase happened, so it can be restored on a new phone. We never see your payment card details.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Solodoku stores your progress (levels solved, times, hints used), your settings, and automatic backup copies of that data on your device. This data stays on your device unless you choose one of the options below. Uninstalling the app removes it.

3. Google Play Games Services (Cloud Save)

On Android, Solodoku can use Google Play Games Services to save your progress to your Google account so it follows you between your devices.

  • Sign-in happens through Google's own Android services. We never see your Google account password or email address.
  • Google identifies your saved game using your Play Games player ID. The saved game contains your progress and settings.
  • The saved game is stored and managed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not host it and cannot read it.
  • Cloud save is optional. The app works fully without signing in.

4. In-App Purchase

Solodoku offers a one-time purchase that unlocks the full game.

  • Payment is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or bank details.
  • We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy) to confirm and restore your purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase status and basic device information needed to validate the purchase. It does not receive your name or email address from us.
  • We receive order and entitlement information from Google and RevenueCat so we can see that a purchase is valid. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Optional Cloud Sync (Advanced)

Some versions of Solodoku include an optional "Cloud Sync" setting for advanced users. It is off by default. If you turn it on, you supply your own Firebase project, and your progress is sent to that project, which you control. It is not operated by us, and we have no access to it. The Android version distributed on Google Play uses Google Play Games for cloud save instead.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID. Solodoku does not use third-party advertising or analytics services. Google may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports through Google Play (Android vitals); these do not identify you.

7. Data Deletion Instructions

We do not hold an account for you, so there is nothing for us to delete in an account of our own. You can remove your data as follows:

  • Data on your device: uninstall the app, or use the reset option in the app's Data menu.
  • Cloud save: open the Settings app on your Android device, go to Google > Settings for Google apps > Play Games, choose Delete Play Games account & data, and under "Delete individual game data" find Solodoku and tap Delete. This data is managed entirely by Google.
  • Purchase record: Google and RevenueCat keep purchase records for their own legal and accounting purposes. To ask for the RevenueCat record linked to your purchase to be deleted, email us at the address below and we will submit the request.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page and update the effective date above.
