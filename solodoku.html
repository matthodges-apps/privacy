1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.
Solodoku is a standalone logic puzzle game. The app does not require you to create an account, does not track your location, and does not secretly transmit data to our servers.

2. Google Play Games Services (Cloud Save)

For users on Android, Solodoku integrates with Google Play Games Services to securely back up your save data.

  • This sign-in process happens entirely through Google's secure native Android services.
  • We never see, process, or have access to your Google account password or email address.
  • Your save data is stored directly in your personal Google Drive / Google Play Games hidden app data, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy. We do not host or have access to this data.

3. Data Deletion Instructions

Since we do not collect or store any of your personal data on our servers, there is no account to delete with us.

Your cloud save data is stored natively in your Google Account via Google Play Games Services. To delete this data:

  1. Open the Settings app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to Google > Settings for Google apps > Play Games.
  3. Select Delete Play Games account & data.
  4. Under "Delete individual game data", find Solodoku and tap Delete.

Because this data is managed entirely by Google, deletion is immediate and we retain no copies or backups.

4. Third-Party Services

Solodoku does not use third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics) and does not display third-party advertisements.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
